Questions we hear often

What does KEMET EG do?

KEMET EG is an integrated engineering and construction firm. We handle architectural, interior, structural and MEP design, BIM, construction and finishing, steel structures, concrete works and firefighting systems.

Can you handle both design and construction?

Yes. Design and execution run under one management, so design coordination, cost, site operations and schedule stay under one control.

Which types of projects do you deliver?

Industrial facilities and warehouses, administrative buildings, medical and commercial spaces, religious buildings and residential interiors.

Where do you work?

Our projects are in Egypt, including Obour City, 10th of Ramadan City, 6th of October City, New Cairo, Giza and Ismailia.
